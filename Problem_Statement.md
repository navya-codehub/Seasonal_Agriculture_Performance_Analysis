# Problem Statement

## Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

Agricultural performance varies across different crops, seasons, locations, irrigation methods, and environmental conditions. It is difficult to identify which factors contribute to better crop yield, production, profitability, efficient water usage, and lower disease and pest risk. Farmers and agricultural decision-makers need better insights into these factors to make informed decisions.

Therefore, this project aims to analyze agricultural data to identify patterns and relationships in crop yield, production, profitability, irrigation efficiency, resource utilization, and disease/pest risk across different crops, seasons, states, and districts. The analysis will help identify better-performing crops and agricultural practices and provide useful insights for improving overall agricultural performance.
